About

From visual scoring to reliable multimodal understanding.

I approach visual assessment as a reasoning problem: what evidence is present, how modalities complement one another, and how a model behaves when labels, image quality, or data distributions change.

I earned an MSc in Computing and IT Management with Distinction from Cardiff University in 2024, supervised by Dr Wei Zhou, and a BSc (Hons) in Computing with First Class Honours from the University of Greenwich in 2023.

My first-author BMVC 2025 work introduced cross-modal scene semantic alignment for image complexity assessment. I later contributed to a Displays paper that uses image-to-text and image-to-video generation to reveal implicit motion in static imagery.

My current project studies degradation-aware vision-language quality assessment, with emphasis on limited labels, difficult samples, generalisation, and stable evaluation. I am now seeking a funded 2027 PhD where this foundation can grow into AIGC analysis, multimodal reasoning, and robust visual perception.
